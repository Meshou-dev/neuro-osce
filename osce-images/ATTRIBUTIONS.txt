ORBIT MRI — ACUTE OPTIC NEURITIS (OSCE teaching, added 2026-08-09)
----------------------------------------------------------------
Purpose: candidate-interpreted teaching image for a LEFT optic neuritis
station. All three files below were DOWNLOADED, opened, and visually
verified at full 1024px resolution AND re-checked under 3.2x brightness +
autocontrast (corner/edge watermark scan). "Clean" = single panel, NO
burned-in text, NO L/R or scanner (TR/TE) overlay, NO arrows, NO ROI
boxes/circles, NO ruler, NO comparison montage, NO watermark.

Licence note: Radiopaedia case images are CC BY-NC-SA 3.0. Radiopaedia's
licence page lists "giving a lecture or tutorial in your department" as a
DEFINITELY-PERMITTED use, so an internal/departmental OSCE teaching station
is fine WITH the attribution string. Do NOT place these into any paywalled
product (e.g. the paid Hakim Platform) — that is NOT permitted under the
NC clause. (User confirmed licence is not a constraint for this private,
internal teaching use; recorded here as good practice.)

Radiological convention applies (no burned-in L/R marker on any file):
image-LEFT = patient's RIGHT, image-RIGHT = patient's LEFT.

[A] orbit-optic-neuritis-coronal-t2fs-left.jpg   ***PRIMARY (non-contrast interpretation)***
    Plane/seq : Coronal T2-weighted, FAT-SATURATED (orbits)
    Side      : LEFT optic nerve affected
    Shows     : Swollen, T2-hyperintense LEFT optic nerve with effacement
                of the bright CSF within the optic-nerve sheath. The normal
                RIGHT optic nerve retains the intact "donut" (thin bright
                CSF ring around a dark nerve) — the two-nerve asymmetry is
                the teaching point.
    Pixels    : 1024 x 1024 (JPEG, grayscale)
    Source URL: https://radiopaedia.org/cases/optic-neuritis-16   (rID 160621)
                Case: 40M, 3 days of painful left eye vision loss; Radiopaedia
                calls it "the classic appearance of optic neuritis."
    Image file: https://prod-images-static.radiopaedia.org/images/61111487/9e107bd5523b02029bbb6eb4fdd5f388e64e33bd38d4abbd98652b7e4bccc1a6_jumbo.jpeg

    Clean?    : YES — verified full-res + brightness/corner scan. No text/arrows/ROI/ruler/overlay/watermark.

[B] orbit-optic-neuritis-coronal-t1post-left.jpg   ***SUPPORTING (post-contrast / enhancement)***
